Well, I guess this is it - finally getting around to adding some of my "written work" to my website. It will be nice to centralize the poems, because they are currently scattered across notebooks, scraps of paper, facebook, etc. But additionally it will be a nudge to write more often (and aparently outside of this damn antigravity IDE with its continuous AI suggestions, I can't even look at the screen as I type this without being bombarded with the bots next best suggestion).

Not feeling like saying much of anything, doing anything, or being anything today to be frank. But first order emotions and all that, it'll pass. It always does. 

I hate this

I hate that I miss the taste of your lips

I hate that instead of leaning in for a kiss

I'm sitting here letting my mind reminisce
