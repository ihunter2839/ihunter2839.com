title: "Divisions"
author: "Ian Hunter"
date: 2016-09-09

Trying not to see the sides \
Just to focus on the division \
People have split themselves in two \
Based on just one night's decision \
And although the incision has cut to bone \
This place, \
The structure that we call home \
Can't go on in shambles. \
With two half bodies that only speak in \
Yelling matches \
Brawling over a light and a match stick \
To turn us all to ashes. \
So while I'm concerned and confused \
I refuse to spew verbal abuse \
Upon those with differing views. \
If you think that only your side is afraid \
Please think again. \
If you think that only one side of this is wrong \
Then there's a record to amend. \
Trying not to see the sides, \
Just focus on the division. \
And how to erase away these lines \
That tear apart all our visions. \
