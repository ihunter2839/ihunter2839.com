An easy homemade chili recipe made with ground beef, onion, tomatoes, beans, and seasonings. Cooks in just 20 minutes for a crowd-pleasing meal.

Originally from [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/16248/easy-homemade-chili/)

### Ingredients

- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 (15 ounce) can tomato sauce
- 1 (15 ounce) can kidney beans
- 1 (14.5 ounce) can stewed tomatoes
- 1 ½ cups water, or as needed (Optional)
- 1 teaspoon chili powder, or more to taste
- 1 pinch garlic powder
- salt and pepper to taste

### Directions

1. **Cook Beef and Onion**: Place ground beef and onion in a large saucepan over medium heat; cook and stir until meat is browned and onion is tender, about 5 to 7 minutes.

2. **Simmer**: Stir in tomato sauce, kidney beans, and stewed tomatoes with juice. If you prefer a thinner consistency, you can add water. Season with chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, cover and let simmer for 15 minutes.

### Cook's Note
I like to serve this chili with cornbread or bowls of toppings, such as shredded cheese, sour cream, tortilla chips, sliced jalapeños, and green onions, so that people can help themselves.
