Burgeoning Bouquets

May 17, 2017 • Ian Hunter

Burgeoning bouquets,
count the petals before they wither.
Think about you as I watch sunflowers through my window.
Blowing gently in the breeze as
busy bees inspect the seeds
collecting pollen on their backs until
they leave and don’t come back.

Blossoming arrays outside
bring memories of days gone by.
We’ll plant them in the spring I said
you laughed it off, just shook your head
And as we stood there knowing in full
That all flowers bloom
but quickly are pulled
back to the ground where
all the living things are planted.
I dig the hole alone
drop the seeds in empty handed.
